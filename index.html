01

Tandem tilt-rotor VTOL UAV

Undergraduate thesis, 2023–24. Structural model validated to 0.66% deformation error.

Problem
Fixed-wing drones are fast but need runways. Rotary-wing drones hover but are slow. This design combines vertical take-off with efficient forward flight.
My role
Design, analysis and fabrication in a team of three, supervised by Ziad Bin Abdul Awal. NEEDED: your specific share
Method
SolidWorks 2021 CAD; NACA 6412 wing; ANSYS static structural (E-glass/epoxy skin, steel rods); ANSYS Fluent with a poly-hex mesh on a half model; 3D-printed subscale model (225 mm span) in the AF1450 subsonic wind tunnel.
Result
Simulation: CL 0.8082, CD 0.24, CM −0.292 NEEDED: AoA and airspeed
Validation errors: deformation 0.66%, CD 0.12%, CL 13.7%.
Limit
Wind-tunnel results were unsatisfactory because of rig errors. Next step: repeat the tests on a corrected setup.
